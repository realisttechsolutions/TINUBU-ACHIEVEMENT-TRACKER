## 3. Methodology Audits & Structural Breaks

### A. 2025 CPI Rebasing Exercise
- **Old Base**: 2009=100, 740 basket items, 2003/2004 expenditure survey weights (51.8% food weighting).
- **New Base**: 2024=100, 934 basket items, 2023 NLSS weights, 13 COICOP 2018 divisions.
- **Analytical Implication**: The rebased series recalibrated the weight of goods to reflect modern consumption habits. Official headline inflation registered at 22.97% (May 2025) and moderated to 15.43% (July 2026). Food inflation has accelerated for six consecutive months in 2026 (reaching 20.31% in July 2026 with MoM at 5.56%), reflecting persistent household food cost pressures.
- **Visual Rule**: PTAT charts must not draw an unbroken seamless line across the 2024/2025 transition without an explicit methodology break badge.

### B. Foreign Exchange Market Unification (June 2023)
- **Pre-Reform Regime**: Multiple segmented windows with an artificial official peg at ₦461.50/$1 alongside a parallel market trading at ~₦760/$1 (spread >60%).
- **Post-Reform Regime**: Market-driven autonomous price discovery on the NAFEM window with volume-weighted rate reporting. By August 2026, the NAFEM rate stood at ₦1,350.41/$1 with the parallel market premium compressed to <4%.

### C. Sovereign Public Debt Dynamics & FX Translation

  - Total Public Debt: **₦159.35158144 Trillion (US$114.95440 Billion)**
  - Domestic Debt: **₦89.5200 Trillion (56.18%)**
