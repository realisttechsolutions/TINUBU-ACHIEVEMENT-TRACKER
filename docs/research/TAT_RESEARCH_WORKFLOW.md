# Research Discovery Lifecycle & Workflow — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document outlines the 14-stage repeatable research workflow for human researchers and AI subagents.

---

## 1. The 14 Workflow Stages

```
1. Research Question Definition
2. Source Discovery (Official portals, legal records, statistics)
3. Source Capture (Bibliographic recording & archive URL capture)
4. Entity Identification (New policy/project/programme vs existing record)
5. Claim Extraction (Extracting atomic factual assertions)
6. Structured Classification (Assigning sector, status, classification, geography)
7. Evidence Linking (Connecting claims to sources with explicit roles)
8. Duplicate Check (Matching against existing canonical slugs & aliases)
9. Contradiction Check (Flagging conflicting dates/metrics)
10. Verification Review (Assigning verification status & evidence profile)
11. Schema Validation (Automated JSON Schema & CSV linting)
12. Editorial Review (Applying 13 Truth Rules & public copy audit)
13. Human Approval (Senior Editor sign-off on Medium/High/Critical risk claims)
14. Engineering Handoff Preparation (validated staging package for an approved implementation)
```
