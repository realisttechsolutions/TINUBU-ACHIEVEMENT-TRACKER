# Source Role Standards — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document defines the 11 explicit relationship-level source roles attached to claim-source links.

---

## 1. The 11 Relationship Source Roles

1. `primary`: Direct statutory or legal record establishing the claim.
2. `official_statistical`: Empirical statistical measurement from NBS/CBN/DMO.
3. `direct_implementation`: On-site inspection or engineering completion report.
4. `independent_assessment`: Verification conducted by un-affiliated third party.
5. `corroborating`: Secondary source confirming details of a primary claim.
6. `supporting`: Contextual background material.
7. `contextual`: Broader sectoral or economic framework.
8. `contradictory`: Credible source presenting conflicting data or timelines.
9. `replacement`: Updated source replacing a superseded figure.
10. `archived`: Historical record preserved for audit trails.
11. `discovery_lead`: Initial lead guiding research investigation.

---

## 2. Claim-Bound Dynamic Roles
A source does not have a static role across the platform; its role is dynamically assigned per claim link. For instance, a law is `primary` for legal enactment, but only `contextual` for physical project progress.
