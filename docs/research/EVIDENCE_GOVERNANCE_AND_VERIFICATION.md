# Evidence Governance, Verification Standards & Research Operating Model — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document defines the authoritative evidence governance framework, claim verification rules, contradiction protocols, data freshness rules, research risk classifications, and multi-agent human/AI operating model for the **Tinubu Achievement Tracker (TAT) V2**.

---

## 1. Source Hierarchy & Evidential Roles

### 1.1 Source Hierarchy (Levels 1–6)

| Level | Source Level Name | Eligible Source Types | Evidential Authority | Primary vs Discovery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Primary Legal & Administrative Records | Official Gazettes, Executive Orders, Acts of National Assembly, FEC Resolutions, Signed Budgets, Court Records, Statutory Instruments. | **Highest Authoritative** | Primary Evidence |
| **Level 2** | Official Statistical & Institutional Data | National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), Debt Management Office (DMO), Budget Office, Federal Ministries/Agencies datasets. | **Empirical Statutory** | Primary Evidence |
| **Level 3** | International & Independent Institutional Evidence | World Bank Group, IMF, African Development Bank (AfDB), OPEC, United Nations agencies, Independent Audits, Peer-Reviewed Policy Journals. | **High Independent** | Primary / Corroborating |
| **Level 4** | Credible Mainstream & Specialist Media | Premium Times, Channels TV, Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Arise News, Daily Trust, Punch. | **Secondary Empirical** | Supporting Corroboration |
| **Level 5** | Official Press Statements & Speeches | Presidential Media Statements, Ministry Press Releases, Briefing Notes, Speeches, Official Social Media. | **Direct Statement** | Direct Announcement Evidence |
| **Level 6** | Discovery Leads | Social Media Posts, Community Blogs, Unverified Snippets, Aggregators. | **Discovery Only** | Discovery Lead ONLY (Must Not Publish Directly) |

---

### 1.2 Relationship-Level Source Roles

A source does not possess a static weight in isolation; its authority is bound to a specific claim via one of 11 explicit **Source Roles**:

1. `primary`: Direct statutory or official record establishing the claim.
2. `official_statistical`: Empirical statistical measurement from NBS/CBN/DMO.
3. `direct_implementation`: On-site project inspection, engineering sign-off, or physical delivery report.
4. `independent_assessment`: Verification conducted by an un-affiliated third party (World Bank, independent audit).
5. `corroborating`: Secondary source confirming details of a primary claim.
6. `supporting`: Contextual background material.
7. `contextual`: Broader sectoral or economic framework.
8. `contradictory`: Credible source presenting conflicting data or timelines.
9. `replacement`: Updated source replacing a superseded figure.
10. `archived`: Historical record preserved for version audit.
11. `discovery_lead`: Initial lead guiding research investigation.

## 2. Claim-Level Evidence Standard & Verification Statuses

Every public claim in the platform is decomposed into an atomic evidence node with explicit verification fields:

### 2.1 Verification Statuses

- `Source Confirmed`: Claim matches Level 1-2 primary source document.
- `Cross-Referenced`: Verified across 2+ distinct Level 1-4 sources.
- `Independently Corroborated`: Level 1-2 government statement backed by Level 3 independent institutional evidence.
- `Government Reported`: Official MDA figure lacking independent verification (clearly labeled as Government Reported).
- `Provisional`: Preliminary statistic subject to scheduled NBS/CBN revision.
- `Under Review`: Audit flagged due to conflicting figures or data updates.
- `Corrected`: Publicly updated following formal revision log.
- `Withdrawn`: Retracted due to policy revocation or source error.

---

## 3. Evidence Profiles

Records are assigned an overarching **Evidence Profile** reflecting their cumulative evidence backing:

1. `Primary Official Only`: Supported exclusively by Level 1-2 statutory records.
2. `Official Statistical`: Supported by official empirical statistical bulletins.
3. `Official and Supporting`: Level 1-2 primary evidence plus Level 4 media context.
4. `Official and Independent`: Level 1-2 government record corroborated by Level 3 international/independent data.
5. `Independent Primary`: Primary evidence generated by an independent body.
6. `Mixed with Contradiction`: Contains unresolved material discrepancies across sources (requires qualification note).
7. `Limited Evidence`: Single Level 4-5 source (restricted to `publishable-with-qualification`).
8. `Under Review`: Incomplete evidence verification in progress.

---

## 4. Contradiction-Handling Protocol

When conflicting figures, dates, or statuses are discovered across sources, researchers MUST NOT select the most favorable number or delete competing claims.

### 4.1 Contradiction Severity Matrix

| Severity | Definition | Resolution Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minor** | Typographical date variance or rounding difference (e.g. 47km vs 47.2km). | Document in `internal_notes`; publish rounded canonical figure. |
| **Material** | Significant metric variance (e.g. 5,000 vs 8,500 beneficiaries) or status disagreement. | Preserve both in internal log; publish conservative figure with explicit qualification note. |
| **Critical** | Fundamental contradiction on delivery status, legal validity, or funding release. | Revert record status to `under-review`; block public display until senior editor audit. |

## 5. Duplicate-Detection Standard

To prevent duplicate project or policy entries, the ingestion pipeline checks:
- **Exact Matches**: Canonical slug, Gazette reference number, contract ID.
- **Fuzzy Matches**: Project title similarity > 85%, identical MDA + identical state/zone.
- **Action**: Signal candidate duplicate -> flag for human reviewer merge -> preserve aliases in `alias_titles`.

## 6. Data Freshness Policy

| Velocity Class | Target Domains | Maximum Stale Threshold | Review Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Velocity** | FX Rates, Inflation, Reserves, Security Statistics | Monthly | New NBS/CBN Monthly Bulletin |
| **Medium Velocity** | Quarterly GDP, Capital Importation, Student Loan Disbursements | Quarterly | Quarterly NBS Report |
| **Low Velocity** | Infrastructure Construction, Legislation, Executive Orders | Bi-Annually / Annual | Mid-Year Budget Review |
| **Event-Driven** | Policy Announcements, Commissioning Ceremonies | Immediate | Official Gazette / FEC Resolution |

## 7. Human & AI Research Operating Model

To maintain maximum evidence integrity, research is conducted via a controlled 11-role multi-agent workflow:

```
[ Research Planner ] ──> [ Source Discovery Agent ] ──> [ Source Capture Agent ]
                                                               │
                                                               ▼
[ Verification Agent ] <── [ Entity Resolution Agent ] <── [ Claim Extraction Agent ]
          │
          ▼
[ Reconciliation Agent ] ──> [ QA Agent ] ──> [ Editorial Agent ] ──> [ Human Reviewer ] ──> [ Data Publisher ]
```

### Inviolable Operating Rule
**No single AI agent may discover, extract, verify, and approve a record for publication without independent QA check and human editorial sign-off for Medium, High, and Critical risk records.**

---

## 8. Research Risk Classification

- **Low Risk**: Public legislative titles, MDA names, gazetted dates. (Requires 1 Level 1-2 source).
- **Medium Risk**: Programme status, state coverage, contract values. (Requires 2 sources; Level 1-2 + Level 3-4).
- **High Risk**: Security casualty/incident reductions, poverty alleviation metrics, macro employment creation claims. (Requires Level 2 statutory data + Level 3 independent corroboration + Senior Human Reviewer sign-off).
- **Critical Risk**: Sensitive security operations, legal disputes, major fiscal expenditure claims. (Requires Level 1 Gazette/FEC Act + Level 2 Statutory Report + Dual Senior Human Approval).
