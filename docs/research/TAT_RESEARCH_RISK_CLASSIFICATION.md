# Research Risk Classification — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document defines 4 risk levels governing source requirements.

---

## 1. The 4 Risk Levels
- **Low Risk**: Gazetted legislative titles, official MDA names. (1 Level 1-2 source required).
- **Medium Risk**: Programme rollout status, contract values. (2 Level 1-4 sources required).
- **High Risk**: Security outcomes, macro employment metrics. (Level 2 Statutory + Level 3 Independent Data + Senior Human Approval).
- **Critical Risk**: Sensitive security operations, fiscal deficit figures. (Level 1 Gazette + Level 2 Statutory Report + Dual Senior Human Approval).
