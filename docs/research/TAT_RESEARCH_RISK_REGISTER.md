## Master Risk Register

| Risk ID | Category | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Trigger / Indicator | Preventive Control & Mitigation | Owner | Residual Risk | Contingency Protocol |
|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|---|---|:---:|---|
| **R-01** | Data Availability | Insufficient official data published by implementing MDAs. | High | High | Research backlog tickets in `data_gap.csv`. | Broad multi-tier sourcing (multilateral reports, legislative gazettes, NBS releases). | Research Planner | Medium | Escalate via FOI request or mark record as "Qualified / Incomplete". |
| **R-02** | Evidence Integrity | Broken government portal URLs and link rot. | High | Medium | HTTP 404/500 errors during periodic URL link check. | Mandate web archive snapshots (Wayback Machine / Perma.cc) in `source_capture.csv`. | Source Capture Agent | Low | Auto-redirect public citation to verified archive snapshot. |
| **R-03** | Factual Conflict | Conflicting official statistics published by different MDAs. | High | High | Discrepancy detected during multi-source extraction. | Log in `contradiction_log.csv`; apply conservative official figure or Level 2 bulletin. | Reconciliation Agent | Medium | Publish qualification note displaying both figures transparently. |
| **R-04** | Staleness | Outdated project or progress metrics displayed on public portal. | Medium | Medium | Exceeded freshness velocity threshold (e.g. >90 days). | Automated freshness review scheduler (`freshness_review.csv`). | Verification Agent | Low | Flag record as "Due for Review" on public interface. |
| **R-05** | Deduplication | Duplicate achievement records created under alternate titles. | Medium | Medium | Duplicate detection similarity score >0.85. | Automated exact key checks + fuzzy match reviews in `duplicate_review.csv`. | Entity Resolution Agent | Low | Merge duplicate candidate into primary canonical ID. |
| **R-06** | Status Classification | Misclassifying an announcement or approval as completed delivery. | High | Critical | Record tagged `completed` without completion certificate. | Strict gate enforcement of 21-stage implementation transitions. | Editorial Agent | Very Low | Immediate rejection in Gate 4; reclassify to `announced`/`ongoing`. |
| **R-07** | Financial Misclassification | Aggregating approved contract envelope with actual cash releases. | High | Critical | Financial record summing across unlike categories. | 11 canonical financial types; NUMERIC(24,4) schema; strict aggregation rules. | Schema / QA Agent | Very Low | Reject batch during dry-run; enforce distinct financial typing. |
| **R-08** | Beneficiary Misclassification | Conflating registered applicants with paid disbursement recipients. | High | Critical | Student loan portal applicants reported as paid loans. | 6 distinct beneficiary stages; require payment platform verification. | Extraction Agent | Very Low | Reclassify to `applicant` or `approved_beneficiary`. |
| **R-09** | Circular Sourcing | Media outlets echoing single uncorroborated government press release. | High | Medium | Multiple Level 4 articles matching single Level 5 text. | Audit source origins; require independent primary or Level 2/3 evidence. | Verification Agent | Low | Downgrade verification status from `cross_referenced` to `source_confirmed`. |
| **R-10** | AI Hallucination | AI extraction agent generating plausible but ungrounded facts. | Medium | Critical | Discrepancy between extracted text and source locator. | Strict atomic extraction with mandatory page/paragraph locators + human sign-off. | Human Reviewer | Very Low | Immediate task quarantine and researcher re-audit. |
| **R-11** | Causal Overreach | Unsubstantiated causal attribution of macro shifts solely to executive policy. | Medium | High | Broad causal claims in description text. | Enforce neutral, objective drafting in Gate 4; focus on demonstrable outputs. | Lead Editor | Low | Edit text to describe factual action without over-claiming causation. |
| **R-12** | Selection Bias Pressure | Pressure to overstate delivery speed or impact metrics. | Medium | High | Hyperbolic or promotional wording in draft. | Strict enforcement of the 13 Truth Rules and Editorial Charter. | Editorial Board | Very Low | Editorial rejection and mandatory revision. |
| **R-13** | National Security | Accidental publication of sensitive defense coordinates or tactical data. | Low | Critical | Security-related achievement with precise geospatial point. | Mandatory security review gate for defense assets; redact coordinates to state/national scope. | Compliance Officer | Very Low | Immediate retraction and database record redaction. |
| **R-14** | Copyright Infringement | Over-collection of full-text copyrighted media or research publications. | Medium | Medium | Large text blocks stored in evidence summaries. | Store only metadata, locators, and researcher summaries (<500 chars). | Source Capture Agent | Very Low | Trim text to concise locator and direct link. |
| **R-15** | Source Deletion | Primary official source taken down following administrative changes. | Medium | High | Source URL becomes unreachable and unarchived. | Pre-emptive archive capture at Gate 1; compute SHA-256 hash. | Source Capture Agent | Low | Mark source as `archived` with verified historical hash. |
| **R-16** | Schema Drift | Inconsistent enum codes used between research batches and database. | Medium | High | AJV validation errors during batch ingestion. | Sole authoritative `canonical-vocabulary.v1.1.2.json` + exact bidirectional validation. | Schema / QA Agent | Very Low | Automated CI build failure blocks non-conforming batches. |
| **R-17** | Engineering Misalignment | Research contracts assuming vendor-specific database or authorization features. | Low | High | Divergence between research requirements and the approved engineering implementation. | Neutral research contracts; engineering ownership of implementation mechanisms. | Tech Lead | Very Low | Alignment review prior to each engineering mission. |
| **R-18** | Review Backlog | Volume of extracted claims exceeding human reviewer sign-off capacity. | High | Medium | Unreviewed queue in Gate 4 growing beyond SLA. | Multi-tier reviewer distribution; prioritize critical/high risk queues. | Research Lead | Medium | Reallocate reviewer capacity or throttle extraction velocity. |
| **R-19** | Translation Inconsistency | Inconsistent translation of local currency values (e.g. Billion vs Trillion). | Low | High | Ambiguous numerical strings in CSV rows. | Require exact numerical base decimal values in `amount` (e.g. 47000000000.00). | Schema / QA Agent | Very Low | Schema validation error halts ingestion. |
| **R-20** | Untracked Revisions | Revisions to published records executed without public audit trail. | Low | High | Unlogged changes to published figures. | Mandatory `corrections` and immutable `record_versions` logging. | Compliance Officer | Very Low | Publish transparent correction notice on record detail page. |
| **R-21** | Security Misconfiguration | Public connector operations exposing internal researcher notes or emails. | Low | Critical | Public GraphQL query returning internal fields. | Strict connector projection rules in Firebase SQL Connect; test response shapes. | Security Engineer | Very Low | Deploy updated connector configuration immediately. |
| **R-22** | Credential Compromise | Privileged backend credential exposed in repository or logs. | Low | Critical | Secret detected in git commits or error traces. | Use short-lived managed identity and secret-management controls; no static keys in source. | DevOps Lead | Very Low | Immediate credential revocation and access-policy rotation. |
| **R-23** | Importer Partial Commit | Ingestion failure leaving orphaned claims or records in database. | Low | High | Database transaction abortion mid-batch. | Atomic PostgreSQL transactions; logical records commit completely or roll back. | Ingestion Engineer | Very Low | Automatic transactional rollback to pre-batch state. |
| **R-24** | Hosting Cost Escalation | Uncontrolled database sizing or query volume. | Medium | High | Monthly billing threshold alerts triggered. | Start with cost-appropriate capacity, caching, and local development; engineering owns vendor controls. | Cloud Architect | Low | Optimize indexes and adjust approved infrastructure capacity. |
| **R-25** | Database Backup Failure | Inability to restore database following accidental corruption or data loss. | Low | Critical | Backup job failure or failed restore drill. | Automated backups, governed point-in-time recovery, and quarterly restore drills as engineering requirements. | Database Owner | Very Low | Restore to a verified recovery timestamp using the approved platform. |
| **R-26** | Storage Bucket Exposure | Private evidentiary files publicly accessible via unauthenticated URL. | Low | High | Bucket permissions set to public-read. | Strict bucket separation (public assets vs private restricted evidence). | Cloud Architect | Very Low | Re-apply private IAM access control on evidence bucket. |
| **R-27** | Official Site Format Change | Automated scraper breaking due to redesign of government portal. | High | Low | Scraper error rate spike. | Decouple extraction from scraping; prioritize standard PDF/Gazette downloads. | Extraction Agent | Low | Update site parser or switch to manual document download. |
| **R-28** | Vendor Deprecation | Upstream changes to Firebase SQL Connect or Google Cloud services. | Low | Medium | Service deprecation notice from vendor. | Standardized PostgreSQL system of record; portable schema abstractions. | Tech Lead | Very Low | Standard PostgreSQL migration if required. |
