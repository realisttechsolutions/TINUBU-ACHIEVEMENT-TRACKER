# Research Objectives — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document defines the core strategic and operational research objectives across 5 primary categories.

---

## 1. Public Communication Objectives
- Provide transparent, evidence-backed public reporting on government actions, physical infrastructure delivery, social interventions, and economic policy reforms.
- Clearly present the exact implementation stage of every project or policy (avoiding presenting announcements as delivery).
- Provide regional and state-level geographic attribution to illustrate nationwide impact across all 36 states and FCT.
- Link every public claim directly to authoritative Level 1–5 source citations.

---

## 2. Evidence Objectives
- Enforce strict 1:1 traceability between public statements and primary legal/statutory evidence.
- Maintain accurate reporting periods, financial classifications (approval vs funding release vs expenditure), and beneficiary classifications (applicants vs active recipients).
- Preserving internal visibility for contradictions, revisions, and historical data updates.

---

## 3. Database Objectives
- Structure all research outputs into normalized, schema-validated database records.
- Enable multi-faceted search, sector filtering, map visualizations, time-series charts, and executive report exports.
- Support seamless CSV/JSON ingestion into Supabase staging tables.

---

## 4. Operational Objectives
- Establish an 11-role human and AI agent operating model with explicit separation of discovery, extraction, verification, and editorial sign-off.
- Eliminate single-agent approval of high-risk claims.

---

## 5. Long-Term Objectives
- Provide a sustainable infrastructure for ongoing data freshness audits, routine quarterly metric updates, historical versioning, public report downloads, and future API integrations.
