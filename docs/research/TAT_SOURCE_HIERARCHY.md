## 1. Authoritative Six-Tier Source Hierarchy

Research Contract v1.1 establishes an authoritative six-tier source hierarchy. Every evidence document captured in the research repository must be classified under exactly one source level:

```text
+───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
|                                6-LEVEL EVIDENCE SOURCE HIERARCHY                                  |
+─────────┬────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| Level   | Category Description                   | Examples & Primary Authority                   |
+─────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| LEVEL 1 | Primary Legal & Administrative Records | Acts of NASS, Official Gazettes, Exec Orders,  |
|         |                                        | FEC Approvals, Signed Bilateral Treaties       |
+─────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| LEVEL 2 | Official Statistical & Admin Data      | NBS Statistical Bulletins, CBN Reports, DMO,   |
|         |                                        | Budget Office Reports, MDA Project Logs        |
+─────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| LEVEL 3 | International & Independent Evidence   | World Bank, IMF, AfDB, UN Agency Reports,      |
|         |                                        | NEITI Audits, Independent Technical Audits     |
+─────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| LEVEL 4 | Credible Mainstream & Specialist Media | Reputable National Dailies, Wire Services,     |
|         |                                        | Peer-Reviewed Journals, Investigative Outlets  |
+─────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| LEVEL 5 | Official Statements & Context          | State House Press Releases, Ministerial        |
|         |                                        | Briefing Transcripts, Agency Portal Notes      |
+─────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| LEVEL 6 | Discovery Leads (INTERNAL ONLY)        | Social Media Feeds, Unverified Public Leads    |
|         |                                        | (STRICTLY PROHIBITED AS PUBLIC PROOF)          |
+─────────┴────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────+
```

## 2. Evidentiary Principles & Epistemic Boundaries

### 2.1 Hierarchy Does Not Guarantee Claim Truth by Itself
A high-tier source proves only what it directly documents:
- **Level 1 Enactment ≠ Implementation:** An enacted statute proves legal authority, but cannot prove successful implementation or operational delivery.
- **Level 5 Statement ≠ Independent Outcome:** A ministerial press release proves that the government announced an initiative, but cannot serve as independent verification of its ultimate public impact.
- **Circular Media Sourcing:** Multiple Level 4 media articles quoting the exact same press briefing do not constitute independent multi-source corroboration.

### 2.2 Level 6 Discovery Isolation
Level 6 sources represent discovery triggers (e.g. social media posts from verified public handles or community tips). They are strictly restricted to the internal research pipeline and **must never be published as public proof citations** without corroboration from Levels 1–5.
