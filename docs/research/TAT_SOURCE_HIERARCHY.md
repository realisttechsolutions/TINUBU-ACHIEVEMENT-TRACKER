# Source Hierarchy & Evidential Standards — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document defines the 6-tier source hierarchy governing data collection.

---

## 1. The 6-Tier Source Hierarchy

| Tier | Tier Name | Eligible Source Types | Trust Weight | Primary vs Discovery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Primary Legal & Administrative | Gazettes, Executive Orders, Signed Acts, FEC Resolutions, Signed Budgets, Court Records. | **Highest** | Primary Evidence |
| **Level 2** | Statutory & Institutional Data | NBS Bulletins, CBN Reports, DMO Releases, Budget Office Warrants, Ministry Reports. | **High** | Primary Evidence |
| **Level 3** | International & Independent | World Bank, IMF, AfDB, OPEC, UN, Independent Audit Reports. | **High** | Independent Evidence |
| **Level 4** | Credible Mainstream Media | Premium Times, Channels TV, Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Arise News. | **Medium** | Secondary Corroboration |
| **Level 5** | Official Press Statements | Media Statements, Speeches, Press Releases, Official Social Media. | **Direct** | Direct Statement |
| **Level 6** | Discovery Leads | Social Media Posts, Unverified Blogs, Snippets. | **None** | Discovery Only (Not Publishable Alone) |

---

## 2. Inviolable Source Rules
- Level 6 discovery leads MUST NOT serve as sole publication evidence for any record.
- Repeated media reproduction of one press release does NOT constitute multiple independent confirmations (Single-Source Circularity Check).
