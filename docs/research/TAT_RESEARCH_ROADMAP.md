# Phased Research Roadmap — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document details the 5-phase research rollout schedule.

---

## 1. The 5 Roadmap Phases
- **Phase 1: Foundation & Blueprint (Mission 01)**: Taxonomies, schemas, CSV templates, validation scripts.
- **Phase 2: Pilot Research (Mission 02)**: Controlled testing across 5 sample records.
- **Phase 3: Core Sector Research (Missions 03-05)**: Mass research across Economy, Security, Infrastructure, Social Services, and Governance.
- **Phase 4: Database Ingestion & CMS Integration (Backend Assignment)**: Supabase migration & ingestion pipeline execution.
- **Phase 5: Continuous Monitoring & Data Freshness**: Scheduled quarterly metric refreshes & revision logs.
