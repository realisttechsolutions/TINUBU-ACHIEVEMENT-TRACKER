# Research Prioritisation Standards — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document establishes the guidelines for research focus and topic selection.

---

## 1. Prioritisation Criteria
Research prioritisation concentrates on records demonstrating:
1. Tangible physical project completion or ongoing construction milestones.
2. Major fiscal, monetary, or structural policy reforms with gazetted backing.
3. High-impact social programmes with active beneficiary disbursements.
4. Institutional digitisation or anti-corruption reforms.
5. Macroeconomic indicator advancements published by NBS/CBN.
