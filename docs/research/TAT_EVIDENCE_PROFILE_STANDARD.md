# Evidence Profile Standards — Tinubu Achievement Tracker V2

This document defines the 8 cumulative record-level evidence profiles.

---

## 1. The 8 Record Evidence Profiles
1. `Primary Official Only`: Supported exclusively by Level 1-2 statutory records.
2. `Official Statistical`: Supported by official empirical statistical bulletins (NBS/CBN).
3. `Official and Supporting`: Level 1-2 primary evidence plus Level 4 media context.
4. `Official and Independent`: Level 1-2 government record corroborated by Level 3 international data.
5. `Independent Primary`: Primary evidence generated by an independent body.
6. `Mixed with Contradiction`: Contains unresolved material discrepancies across sources (requires qualification note).
7. `Limited Evidence`: Single Level 4-5 source (restricted to `publishable_with_qualification`).
8. `Under Review`: Incomplete evidence verification in progress.
