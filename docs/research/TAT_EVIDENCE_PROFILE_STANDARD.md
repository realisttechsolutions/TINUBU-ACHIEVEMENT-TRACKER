## 1. Architectural Role of Evidence Profiles

An **Evidence Profile** is a derived classification assigned to a published achievement record based on the primary nature of its supporting evidence bundle. It provides users and researchers with immediate transparency regarding the evidentiary foundation of any published claim.

---

## 2. The Eight Canonical Evidence Profiles

| # | Profile Code | Public Label | Minimum Evidentiary Requirements | Primary Application Sectors |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `direct_physical_delivery` | Direct Physical Delivery | Physical site verification, contractor completion certificates, handover logs, geocoded imagery. | Infrastructure, Housing, Transport, Power Grid |
| 2 | `statutory_legal_enactment` | Statutory / Legal Enactment | Official Gazette publication, signed Act Number, National Assembly Assent Certificate, signed Executive Order. | Legislation, Legal Reforms, Regulations, Executive Directives |
| 3 | `verified_administrative_disbursement` | Verified Administrative Disbursement | Treasury warrants, payment platform transaction audit logs, banking reconciliation, beneficiary disbursement receipts. | Social Welfare, Student Loans (NELFUND), Consumer Credit (CREDICORP), MSME Grants |
| 4 | `statistical_indicator_movement` | Statistical Indicator Movement | Multi-period empirical time-series data from NBS, CBN, DMO, or international statistical agencies. | Economy & Fiscal, Foreign Trade, Revenue Generation, Agricultural Output |
| 5 | `official_policy_declaration` | Official Policy Declaration | Published National Policy Framework, Federal Executive Council extract, formal ministerial circular. | National Strategies, Sector Frameworks, Ministerial Roadmaps |
| 6 | `third_party_independent_assessment` | Third-Party Independent Assessment | Independent non-governmental evaluation, certified professional audit, academic peer-reviewed evaluation. | Governance Turnaround, Anti-Corruption, Health & Education Outcomes |
| 7 | `multilateral_partner_evaluation` | Multilateral Partner Evaluation | Formal evaluation report or completion assessment from World Bank, IMF, African Development Bank, or UN bodies. | Development Co-Financed Projects, Climate Programs, Macro Reviews |
| 8 | `institutional_reform_milestone` | Institutional Reform Milestone | Official MDA restructuring decree, live digital portal rollout audit, service delivery turnaround metrics. | Civil Service Automation, Customs Modernization, Tax Administration |
