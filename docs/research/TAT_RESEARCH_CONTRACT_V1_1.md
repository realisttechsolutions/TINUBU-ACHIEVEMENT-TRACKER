## 1. Executive Research Scope & Permanent Project Context

The **Tinubu Achievement Tracker (TAT)** is an evidence-first, achievements-focused public information platform documenting the demonstrable accomplishments, policy reforms, physical infrastructure projects, social interventions, and institutional advancements of the administration of President Bola Ahmed Tinubu.

### 1.1 Historical Research Window
The permanent research window is **29 May 2023 through August 2026**. Future research will systematically discover, verify, structure, and publish demonstrable positive achievements initiated, funded, accelerated, or completed during this administration.

### 1.2 Exhaustive Discovery Mandate
The research programme is designed for exhaustive positive coverage across all tiers of the Federal Government of Nigeria:
- Sector by sector (all 15 canonical sectors)
- Ministry by ministry, agency by agency, commission by commission
- Programme by programme, project by project
- State by state (all 36 States and the Federal Capital Territory)

Research will continue across these dimensions until marginal research produces predominantly duplicate or already-catalogued records rather than substantive new achievements.

## 2. Editorial Scope & Uncompromising Truth Standards

The platform maintains a deliberately positive, achievements-focused editorial scope. Future research prioritisation concentrates on records showing tangible delivery, measurable progress, institutional reform, capital investment, operational commissioning, and positive public outcomes.

> [!CRITICAL]
> **POSITIVE SELECTION MUST NEVER WEAKEN FACTUAL ACCURACY.**  
> The editorial scope determines which achievements receive research attention; it does **not** alter the truth standard governing those achievements. Public wording may be confident, engaging, and positive, but it must **never exceed the underlying verifiable evidence**.

### The 13 Core Truth Rules (Mandatory Research Invariants)
1. **Never Fabricate:** Do not create or invent achievements, milestones, or beneficiaries.
2. **Never Inflate Figures:** Report exact numbers as stated in primary official evidence. Never round upwards or exaggerate.
3. **Never Manipulate Reporting Periods:** Preserve the exact fiscal year, quarter, or date range specified in the source.
4. **Announcement ≠ Approval:** An executive announcement or public promise does not prove formal approval.
5. **Approval ≠ Funding:** Federal Executive Council (FEC) or ministerial approval does not prove statutory funding.
6. **Funding ≠ Funding Release:** Budget allocation or approved funding does not prove that cash/warrants were released to the MDA.
7. **Release ≠ Expenditure:** Funds released to a ministry or contractor do not prove actual audited expenditure.
8. **Commencement ≠ Completion:** Project ground-breaking or site mobilization does not prove project delivery.
9. **Completion ≠ Operation:** Civil completion of a facility does not prove it is commissioned, staffed, and operational.
10. **Target ≠ Result:** An administrative target, KPI, or delivery projection must never be presented as an actual outcome.
11. **Applicant ≠ Beneficiary:** Individuals registered on a portal or submitting forms are applicants, not recipients.
12. **Approved ≠ Paid:** Beneficiaries approved for a grant/loan are not disbursement recipients until payment is evidenced.
13. **Government Claim ≠ Independently Verified:** Government-reported statistics must be accurately labelled as government-reported unless genuine independent institutional corroboration exists.

## 5. 21-Stage Implementation Status System

Every record advances through a rigorously defined 21-stage implementation status system. Statuses must never be skipped without satisfying the specific evidentiary threshold:

```text
[proposed] ──> [announced] ──> [approved] ──> [enacted / effective] ──> [funded] ──> [funding_released]
                                                                                            │
[partially_delivered] <── [implementation_ongoing] <── [implementation_planning] <── [procurement]
        │
        └──> [completed] ──> [operational] ──> [outcome_reported] ──> [independently_assessed]
```

### Transition Rules & Evidence Proofs
- `announced ≠ approved`: Proof of announcement (press release) only proves intent; approval requires FEC extract, signed decree, or board resolution.
- `approved ≠ funded`: Approval requires statutory appropriation (Appropriation Act line item) to advance to `funded`.
- `funded ≠ funding_released`: Advancing to `funding_released` requires treasury warrant, cash-backing record, or accountant-general disbursement log.
- `commencement ≠ completed`: Active construction (`implementation_ongoing`) cannot advance to `completed` without certificate of practical completion or engineering handover.
- `completed ≠ operational`: Physical completion requires commissioning and active public utilization to advance to `operational`.
- `operational ≠ outcome_reported`: Operational status proves service availability; `outcome_reported` requires empirical output metrics (e.g. passengers transported, power delivered).
- `outcome_reported ≠ independently_assessed`: Requires external evaluation by World Bank, NEITI, auditor-general, or independent academic audit.

## 6. Source Hierarchy & Claim-Source Relationship Architecture

### 6.1 Six-Level Source Hierarchy
```text
LEVEL 1: Primary Legal and Administrative Records (Acts, Gazettes, Executive Orders, FEC Approvals)
LEVEL 2: Official Statistical and Administrative Data (NBS Bulletins, CBN Reports, DMO Debt Reports)
LEVEL 3: International & Independent Institutional Evidence (World Bank, IMF, AfDB, UN, NEITI Audits)
LEVEL 4: Credible Mainstream and Specialist Media (Reputable national press, verified investigative reports)
LEVEL 5: Official Statements and Contextual Material (Ministerial briefings, State House press releases)
LEVEL 6: Discovery Leads (Social feeds, unverified leads — STRICTLY INTERNAL, NEVER PRIMARY PUBLIC PROOF)
```

### 6.2 Relationship-Bound Source Roles
Source roles are **not static properties of a source document**; they belong to the **Claim ↔ Source relationship**:
- `primary`: The authoritative legal or administrative instrument establishing the claim.
- `official_statistical`: Government statistical data providing baseline or outcome figures.
- `direct_implementation`: Project logs, contractor certificates, or operational telemetry.
- `independent_assessment`: External institutional review evaluating outcome or delivery.
- `corroborating`: Independent secondary source confirming the existence or progress of the claim.
- `supporting`: Background context or baseline metrics.
- `contextual`: Broader sectoral or economic setting.
- `contradictory`: Conflicting, divergent, or disputed evidence logged for transparency.
- `replacement`: Supersedes an outdated or broken evidence citation.
- `archived`: Historical web archive snapshot preserving evidence integrity.
- `discovery_lead`: Initial lead that triggered research investigation.

### 6.3 Claim-Source Architecture (Many-to-Many)
- **One Record** has **Many Claims**.
- **One Claim** has **Many Sources** (via `claim_source_relationships`).
- **One Source** supports **Many Claims**.
- **Same Source** plays **Different Roles** for different claims.
- **Copyright Protection:** Evidence storage records precise locators (`page`, `table`, `section`, `paragraph`) and concise summaries; full-text copyright copying is prohibited.

## 8. Structured Date & Reporting-Period Contract

Imprecise historical dates must never be converted into artificial exact days:
- `date_value`: Nullable ISO calendar date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- `date_precision`: Exactly one of `exact_day`, `month`, `quarter`, `year`, `fiscal_year`, `range`, `unknown`.
- `period_start` / `period_end`: Bounded start and end dates where known.
- `reporting_period_label`: Text label preserving the primary source wording (e.g., "Q2 2024", "FY2023/24").
- `period_is_provisional`: Boolean flag indicating whether the figure is subject to retrospective revision.

## 9. Financial Contract (11 Financial-Value Types)

Financial records must be classified into exactly one of 11 canonical financial types:
1. `budget_allocation`: Appropriated in statutory National Budget.
2. `approved_funding`: Approved by FEC or presidential determination.
3. `funding_released`: Cash-backed funds released to MDA.
4. `reported_expenditure`: Actual audited disbursements to contractors/vendors.
5. `contract_value`: Total formal commercial contract award value.
6. `programme_envelope`: Multi-year programmatic funding envelope.
7. `public_investment`: Direct sovereign capital equity investment.
8. `private_investment`: Private capital mobilized, co-financing, or FDI.
9. `revenue_generated`: Actual tax, customs, or fiscal revenue collected.
10. `revenue_estimate`: Projected future fiscal revenue.
11. `savings_estimate`: Calculated expenditure reduction from reform.

> [!WARNING]
> **AGGREGATION RESTRICTION:** Aggregating unlike financial categories (e.g. summing approved contracts with actual expenditures) is strictly prohibited. Aggregations must group only by identical financial type, identical ISO currency, and non-overlapping reporting periods.

## 10. Beneficiary Contract (6 Beneficiary Stages)

To eliminate misleading beneficiary reporting, records must specify exactly one of 6 lifecycle stages:
1. `applicant`: Entity that submitted an application.
2. `registered_participant`: Captured on programme database or portal.
3. `eligible_applicant`: Formally screened and verified as meeting statutory criteria.
4. `approved_beneficiary`: Screened applicant approved for benefit.
5. `disbursement_recipient`: Verified recipient of cash or in-kind payout.
6. `active_beneficiary`: Entity actively receiving ongoing services or subsidies.

> [!CAUTION]
> **DOUBLE-COUNTING & CROSS-STAGE SUMMING PROHIBITION:**  
> Summing across different stages (e.g. adding applicants to disbursement recipients) is strictly prohibited. Unique cohort identifiers (`cohort_key`) and deduplication notes (`double_counting_note`) are required for all multi-period beneficiary totals.

## 13. Human and AI Separation of Duties

To prevent automated hallucination or unreviewed publication, research and publishing responsibilities are strictly separated across 11 roles:
1. **Research Planner:** Authorizes tasks, scopes, and priorities.
2. **Source Discovery Agent:** Identifies potential primary and secondary evidence leads.
3. **Source Capture Agent:** Captures source metadata, locators, and access dates.
4. **Extraction Agent:** Extracts atomic factual claims into structured templates.
5. **Entity Resolution Agent:** Matches institutions, sectors, and geographies to canonical lookup keys.
6. **Verification Agent:** Cross-references claims against multi-tier evidence.
7. **Reconciliation Agent:** Adjudicates contradictions and duplicate candidates.
8. **Schema / QA Agent:** Executes automated AJV validation and referential dry-runs.
9. **Editorial Agent:** Audits 13 Truth Rules compliance and drafting clarity.
10. **Human Reviewer / Lead Editor:** Mandatory sign-off for publication.
11. **Data Publisher:** Executes privileged transactional import via SQL Connect Admin SDK.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NO SINGLE AI AGENT MAY DISCOVER, EXTRACT, APPROVE, AND PUBLISH A RECORD.**  
> Mandatory human sign-off is required for: critical risk claims, major fiscal values (>₦100B), major beneficiary claims (>500,000), security-related achievements, unresolved contradictions, and legally sensitive matters.

## 15. Ingestion & Database Architecture Boundary

### 15.1 Relational Architecture (27 Logical Tables)
Firebase SQL Connect / Cloud SQL PostgreSQL operates as the primary relational system of record:
- Base `records` table containing core identity, title, summary, status, publication, and audit state.
- Subtype extension tables (`policy_details`, `project_details`, `programme_details`).
- Presentation layer (`achievement_profiles`) for qualifying public achievements.
- Normalized join tables (`record_institutions`, `record_sectors`, `record_geographies`, `record_relationships`, `claim_source_relationships`).
- Time-series and facts tables (`financial_records`, `beneficiary_records`, `indicators`, `indicator_observations`, `timeline_events`).
- Governance & audit tables (`corrections`, `record_versions`, `review_decisions`, `research_batches`).

### 15.2 Ingestion Execution Model
- Ingestion runs as privileged server-side Node.js tooling utilizing the **Firebase SQL Connect Admin SDK**.
- Pipeline modes: `validate` -> `dry-run` -> `stage` -> `commit` -> `rollback`.
- Atomic commits: A logical record (record + extensions + claims + sources + financial/beneficiary rows) commits in a single PostgreSQL transaction or rolls back completely.
