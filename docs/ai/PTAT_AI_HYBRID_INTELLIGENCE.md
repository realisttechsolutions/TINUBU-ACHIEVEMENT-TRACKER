# PTAT AI Hybrid Intelligence Architecture

## Overview

PTAT AI integrates verified local database records from Cloud SQL (`tat-db-staging`) with live Google Search Grounding through Vertex AI (`gemini-3.6-flash`). This hybrid intelligence model ensures that PTAT AI provides authoritative, factual evidence for verified administration achievements while maintaining the natural, responsive capability of a modern AI assistant.

## Core Principles

1. **PTAT-First Grounding**:
   - For facts, initiatives, and metrics represented in the PTAT catalog, PTAT evidence possesses privileged authority.
   - Web grounding supplements gaps or provides recent updates; it does not silently overwrite official records.

2. **Natural AI Conversation**:
   - Interaction is conversational, direct, and unencumbered by repetitive bureaucratic boilerplate.
   - Answers appear immediately at the forefront, with structured provenance and sources disclosed progressively.

3. **Semantic Integrity**:
   - **Financial Truth**: Strict distinctions between funding envelopes, commitments, released funds, disbursements, and expenditures are maintained.
   - **Beneficiary Truth**: Registered learners vs. active trainees vs. certified fellows vs. employed recipients are never conflated.
   - **Geographic Truth**: State-specific physical implementations are distinct from nationwide corridors.
